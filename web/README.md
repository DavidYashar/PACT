## Wallet identities and history

Job parties, bidders and delivery authors resolve usernames from `PactNames.nameOf` on the configured chain. Clicking a username opens that wallet's Blockscout address page (full address and transaction history), not a particular transaction receipt. The adjacent **history** link opens the public PACT profile; its reputation counters come from the on-chain registry and its job list comes from escrow reads.

An open job remains **unassigned** until the employer's `acceptBid` transaction confirms. A bid does not assign its worker.
